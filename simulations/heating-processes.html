Thermal equilibrium & calorimetry

Two bodies in contact exchange thermal energy until they reach the same temperature — the Zeroth Law. Energy lost by the hotter body equals energy gained by the cooler one (assuming no losses). m₁c₁(T᷇−T₁) = −m₂c₂(T᷇−T₂)

Black-body radiation

Every object radiates thermal energy according to its temperature. Stefan's Law gives the total power; Wien's Law gives the peak wavelength — and therefore the perceived colour. P = σAT⁴   λ_max = b/T
